The five phases

The point of the exercise is to find your own findings first. A finding you self-identify and are already remediating is a fundamentally different conversation with a supervisor.

01

Obligation mapping

Build the register of every law, circular, decision and licence condition that binds you, mapped to an internal owner.

Regulatory obligation register
02

Evidence walkthrough

For each obligation, locate the policy, the control, the evidence it operated, and the person accountable. Missing any one is a gap.

Evidence map
03

Gap assessment & scoring

Rate each obligation compliant, partially compliant or non-compliant, with severity weighted by supervisory focus.

Scored gap register
04

Corrective action plan

Every gap gets an action, an owner, a due date and a defined evidence requirement for closure.

Board-approved CAP
05

Mock inspection

Run the actual inspection experience — document requests under time pressure, interviews with control owners, and evidence retrieval.

Mock inspection report

What each authority focuses on

Supervisory attention is not evenly distributed. These are the areas most consistently examined.

Central Bank of Egypt

AML/CFT programme, risk management and capital adequacy, cybersecurity framework, consumer protection SLAs and reporting timeliness.

Financial Regulatory Authority

Licensing conditions, corporate governance code alignment, ESG disclosure completeness and non-banking sector conduct.

Egyptian Exchange

Disclosure timeliness, material event reporting, insider trading controls and governance code compliance.

Money Laundering Combating Unit

STR quality and timeliness, enterprise risk assessment, transaction monitoring calibration and record keeping.

Inspection-day readiness

These are retrieved under time pressure. If retrieval takes days, that itself becomes a finding.

  • Current policies with version control and approval dates
  • Board and committee minutes evidencing oversight
  • The enterprise risk assessment and its approval
  • Training completion records by role
  • Regulatory filings with proof of submission dates
  • Prior findings and documented closure evidence
  • Incident and breach log with response actions
