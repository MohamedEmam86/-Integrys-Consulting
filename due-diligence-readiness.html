The six diligence workstreams

A buyer or investor will test each of these independently. Weakness in one is survivable; weakness across three changes the valuation or kills the deal.

01

Corporate & legal

Commercial register, articles, shareholder agreements, capital history, board minutes and powers of attorney — complete and consistent.

Corporate pack
02

Licensing & regulatory

Every licence current, every condition met, every regulatory correspondence and finding closed or with a live remediation plan.

Regulatory status memo
03

Financial & tax

Audited statements, tax filings and clearances, related party transactions disclosed, and reconciliations that hold.

Financial diligence pack
04

Governance & controls

Board composition and independence, delegated authorities, segregation of duties, and internal audit coverage.

Governance assessment
05

Compliance & financial crime

AML programme, sanctions controls, ABAC framework, whistleblowing channel and training records.

Compliance dossier
06

Technology & data

System inventory, access controls, business continuity, incident history and PDPL data handling.

Technology assessment

Readiness bands

We score each workstream into one of three bands. The band determines whether you open the data room now or in ninety days.

Band A

Ready

Documentation complete, current and internally consistent. Can withstand third-party scrutiny without preparation.

  • Open the data room
Band B

Gaps identified

Core documents exist but with gaps, stale versions or unclosed findings. Remediable within a defined window.

  • 30–90 day remediation
  • Disclose proactively
Band C

Not ready

Material deficiencies that will be discovered and will affect price, terms or completion. Address before engaging counterparties.

  • Do not open yet
  • Structural remediation

The questions that expose an unprepared company

If any of these cannot be answered within a day, the company is not ready.

  • Who are the ultimate beneficial owners, evidenced through every layer?
  • Show every regulatory finding in the last three years and its closure evidence.
  • Which board committees exist, and produce their minutes for the last year.
  • List all related party transactions and the approval for each.
  • Produce the AML enterprise risk assessment and its board approval date.
  • Show the incident log and what changed after each incident.
