PDPL Compliance

How this website aligns with Egypt Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020

Draft for legal review. This page describes how this website aligns with Law 151/2020. It is not a certification of compliance, and it should be reviewed by a qualified Egyptian lawyer.

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 governs the collection and processing of personal data in Egypt. As a firm that advises regulated organisations on data protection, we hold this website to the same standard we recommend to clients. This page sets out, plainly, how integrysconsult.com aligns with the law.

What the PDPL requires, and what we do

RequirementHow this website addresses it
Lawful basis for processingPersonal data is collected only when you voluntarily submit a form. Consent is given by submission, and our legitimate interest is limited to responding to your enquiry.
TransparencyOur Privacy Policy lists every form, the exact fields collected, the purpose, and the retention period.
Data minimisationForms request only what is needed to respond. Phone number, budget and message fields are optional.
Purpose limitationData submitted through a form is used to respond to that enquiry. We do not add you to a marketing list or sell data.
SecurityAll submissions travel over encrypted HTTPS. Data is delivered to a mailbox with restricted access rather than an open database.
RetentionEnquiry data is deleted or anonymised within 24 months of last contact where no engagement follows.
Data subject rightsAccess, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent and objection can be exercised by emailing info@integrysconsult.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
Cross-border transferOur hosting and email providers operate infrastructure outside Egypt. We rely on their contractual safeguards, and we disclose this in the Privacy Policy.
No unnecessary trackingThis website uses no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking pixels.

Data we deliberately do not collect

  • No national ID numbers, passport numbers or government identifiers.
  • No financial account, card or payment data.
  • No sensitive personal data as defined by the PDPL (health, biometric, religious belief, political opinion, criminal record).
  • No behavioural advertising profiles.

If an engagement later requires any of the above, it is handled under a separate written agreement with its own data-handling schedule — never through this website.

Exercising your rights

Email info@integrysconsult.com with the word "PDPL" in the subject line and tell us what you would like us to do. We may need to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Centre established under the PDPL.

Our advisory practice

Integrys advises organisations on PDPL readiness, including data mapping, lawful basis assessment, privacy notices, retention schedules, cross-border transfer arrangements and breach response. That is a separate engagement from this website. See our services or contact us.
