What is inside

Five pillars, one platform

Deploy the modules you need today and add the rest as the compliance function matures. Every module writes to the same risk register, the same control library and the same evidence trail.

01

Financial Crime

AML programme, transaction monitoring with SLA clocks, Arabic-native sanctions and PEP screening, adverse media, eKYC and merchant KYB onboarding.

02

ESG & Carbon

ESG data capture and carbon footprint measurement, connected directly to the enterprise risk register rather than sitting in a separate reporting silo.

03

Governance & Risk

Enterprise risk register with automated KRIs against defined appetite, control library, board fit & proper assessments and the full internal audit lifecycle.

Anonymous whistleblowing intake, CBE-aligned consumer complaint handling with hardcoded 15-day resolution timers, and a dedicated cyber incident hub.

Filings pre-mapped to the Central Bank of Egypt, the Financial Regulatory Authority and the EMLCU, with deadline tracking and calendar export.

Deployment

Host it on your own servers

The reason most Egyptian institutions cannot adopt offshore RegTech is data residency. ComplyNex runs entirely inside your perimeter.

  • Fully hostable on internal servers or private cloud — zero exposure to external SaaS vendors
  • Runs production workloads on 2 vCPUs and 8GB RAM
  • Python (Gunicorn/Flask) workers with a PostgreSQL 16.14 database
  • AES-256 encrypted automated backups and isolated environments
  • Every system action cryptographically logged — exam-ready for CBE inspectors
